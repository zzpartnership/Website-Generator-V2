# Archive

Every losing variant, screenshotted. A good idea for the wrong client is still a good idea.

One folder per variant: `archive/<client>-<name>/` containing `rationale.md`, `tokens.css`, `375.png`, `1440.png`, and the variant's `index.astro`. `/pack` moves the two unshown variants here; the Librarian moves the three unchosen shown ones after the direction locks.

The Adversary and `/audit directions` do not read this folder. `library/shipped.md` is the record of what went live.
