# Taste

Accumulated from real projects. The Art Director reads this before generating a variant.

## Rules for this file

- Every entry names a project and a variant.
- Observations only. No imperatives. The words *prefer*, *always*, *avoid*, *should*, *never* and *must* do not appear in entries.
- Under 100 lines. `/audit taste` proposes cuts.
- Written at the Librarian step from `projects/<client>/reactions.md`.

## Entry format

```
<client> · <variant name> (<constraint level>)
liked:     <what the client pointed at, in their words where possible>
disliked:  <what the client pointed at, in their words where possible>
levi:      <his note>
chosen:    <which variant, with what borrowed from others>
```
